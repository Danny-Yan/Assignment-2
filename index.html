Australia's Vast Agricultural Sector

About 55% of Australia's land is used to grow crops or raise animals, and most of it is grazing country. This page shows where that land is, what it produces and how it has changed between 2010–11 and 2020–21.

Conclusion

Farming covers just over half of Australia, but cropping is only about 5% of the country. Between 2010–11 and 2020–21, cropland grew by almost a third while modified pastures shrank, and the biggest single shift was pasture turning into cropping. Value tells a different story from area: small states can earn as much as huge ones.
